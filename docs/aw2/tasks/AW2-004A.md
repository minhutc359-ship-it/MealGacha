## Acceptance criteria

1. Each image is original and traceable to tool/prompt/source hash. License/review status is recorded honestly; no copied external character/model/style reference or legal clearance claim.
2. World art has up/down/left/right idle/walk frames with stable identity/foot pivot. Battle has idle/move/attack/cast/hit/guard/ultimate/defeat, with visibly distinct poses and declared timing/contact frames.
3. Portrait, world/battle and skill/VFX/audio use separate canonical manifest IDs and `base-v1` art variant. Missing authored effects must not be silently replaced by procedural geometry. Than is melee: no fake projectile.
4. Preserve real alpha for transparent assets, keep frames within declared cells/anchors, avoid clipping/bleed and record source dimensions/packed hashes. Do not claim every animation was generated if motion is interpolated from poses; declare the exact method.
5. The development preview loads manifests, plays the eight battle states/four world directions and all skill cues, with reduced-motion/pause options, and reports missing assets. Production stable entry remains unchanged and excludes development preview.
6. Verify manifest/atlas bounds, actual transparency, asset existence/timing, browser loading/errors and visual captures. Record actual commands/source SHA/environment; remaining full ART-01 12-unit, real combat, physical-device and RIGHTS-01 legal review obligations remain open.
