## Development economy contract (aw2-economy-development-v1)

No currency, purchases, real-money draws or required consumables. Five starters share expedition XP (existing level curve, cap20). Quest grants: 30/50/80/140 XP; ingredient counts rice/herbs/broth: (2,1,0)/(1,0,1)/(0,2,1)/(2,2,2). Pantry chest grants (4,3,2), once per save. One warm bowl consumes one of each ingredient. Sharing one cooked bowl consumes it and grants45 team XP. Cooking/unsharing has no time gate; sharing is optional. Cook/share require the opened hearth or Dì's rest spot; no mandatory story resource costs. All integers bounded; receipts persist with inventory. Completed old quests receive nothing on load; the player explicitly claims eligible grants. These are development tuning values for review, not approved production balance or gacha canon.
