## Development rules contract, before implementation

Profile `aw2-tcg-practice-v1`. Both sides borrow the same fixed20-card composition:3 copies each of five starter unit cards (Than/Lửa Nhỏ/Lam Lam/Mộc Non/Sương Lành),3 Chén ấm and2 Hơi sương. Seeded shuffle per side, opening3 cards and one draw at each own-turn start; player starts. Leader18HP, board3 slots, hand cap7 (overflow discarded), own-turn energy1→6 refreshed per turn. Empty-deck draw causes escalating fatigue1/2/3…; at60 half-turns unresolved game is a draw.

Unit cost/ATK/HP: Than2/1/6 with Hộ vệ; Lửa Nhỏ2/3/2; Lam Lam2/1/3 heals own leader2 on summon; Mộc Non3/2/4 gives each other ally1 shield (cap3); Sương Lành3/2/3 deals1 to every opposing unit on summon. Chén ấm costs1 and heals own leader3 (cannot exceed18 or play at full HP); Hơi sương costs2 and deals2 to a chosen enemy unit. Spells bypass Hộ vệ; normal attacks must target an opposing Hộ vệ while present. Units cannot attack on summon, ready on their next own turn, attack once and exchange simultaneous ATK with unit targets; leader attacks have no retaliation. Damage consumes shields first. All identities/portraits come from existing manifests; these are mode-specific development numbers, not changed RPG kits or approved final stats.

AI uses only its own hand and public board/HP, makes bounded legal moves, then ends turn. A player-controlled one-move recommendation uses the same public/own-hand information. Actual HP/fatigue/turn-limit or explicit surrender determine terminal results; renderer cannot supply outcomes. Rematch is transient and resets only the borrowed match. Match/card/deck state and results never write world save, XP/items/currency/receipts or legacy stores; reload abandons at the adapter checkpoint.
