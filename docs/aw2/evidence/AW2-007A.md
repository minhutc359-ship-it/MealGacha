## Implemented contract

`src/aw2/combat` adds a serializable rules-v1 battle state, development tuning ID, one-to-five unit validation per side, immutable commands, deterministic target selection/fallback, automatic movement/basic/auto skills, manual ultimate commands, queued casts/effects, interruption, shields/healing/debuffs and terminal outcomes. Five starter IDs and skill IDs are canonical; numeric stats are explicitly development tuning, not approved balance.

Melee damage resolves at contact; ranged damage resolves from a queued projectile with its own ID. A cast can fall back before commit if its target is gone; a projectile already launched misses rather than changing target. Both same-tick impacts resolve before terminal settlement, allowing a deterministic draw. Renderer-facing events report outcomes already decided by rules and cannot award damage, victory, loot, quest or save changes.

Starter limits covered: Than shield-expiry/one-basic weaken with cooldown; Lửa Nhỏ two contacts and focus cap; Lam Lam lowest-HP heal and 20% max-HP overheal shield cap; Mộc Non two-distinct-ally cadence and one-charge interrupt guard; Sương Lành three snapshot-area ticks and per-target slow cooldown. Boss/control immunity blocks pull by default. Full balance, renderer, leveling, enemy/boss content, save/reward/world adapter remain outside this source.
