# TCG-005 · Restore the complete card-game loop

Status: COMPLETE (local implementation/automated verification) — 04/11/2026. Base: `32e089cac3a344b760ba110cd415f5326d956afd`.

## Problem and scope

The cutover shipped a preset-unit prototype while removing the reachable collection, deck editor and card economy. Restore a connected TCG, using the existing catalog/art/lore and one Tactical engine: persistent 24-card decks, max two owned copies, four-card opening hand, seeded draw, Lửa Bếp, summon/spell/trap commands, AI card plays, rewards and campaign story.

- Collection: searchable catalog, school/kind/rarity/owned filters, actual Tactical rules, inspect and direct crafting.
- Decks: preserve migrated decks, edit/save/equip multiple 24-card decks, ownership/legality checks and starter recovery.
- Card opening: separate TCG packs, disclosed odds/guarantee, stored reveal/history, two-copy cap and duplicate Tinh Vị. Latest owner request for gacha supersedes the master-plan restriction on random combat packs; Chest's historical data remains preserved.
- Battle: real selected deck/hand/mana, no preset player team in production, reachable win/loss, reaction/Ultimate and persistent replayable checkpoint.
- Narrative: four authored acts, before/after dialogue, node objectives, varied opponents, meaningful ending choice and Manga links.
- Shell: clear home CTA, collection/decks/packs/campaign/guide navigation, responsive hand and card feedback.

## Acceptance and verification

1. A fresh player can inspect the collection, change/equip a legal deck, open a free pack and play those owned cards in a real match.
2. Legacy cards/decks, existing Tactical rewards and original saves survive; invalid/incomplete decks never start a match.
3. Pack/craft/deck/reward/checkpoint writes are validated and persisted before UI applies the result; retries do not duplicate grants.
4. Empty boards do not end a card match; mana, hand/deck/discard, card costs and targets are enforced by the pure reducer.
5. All eight campaign nodes have readable purpose/dialogue/rewards, unlock sequentially and can reach an ending through actual legal plays.
6. Focused rule/economy/migration/simulation tests, full regression, both typechecks, package/build/release checks and browser QA where available.

## Boundaries

No master merge, production deploy, payment or server-ranked PvP. Completion claims must distinguish working local gameplay from unrun device/art/rights checks. Persist documents/status/evidence and push to `game/tactical-tcg`.
