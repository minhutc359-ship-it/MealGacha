## Implemented

- Original `BattleBoard.tsx` hierarchy/`CharacterPortrait` and shared `BattleVfxCanvas` informed the new duel presentation. Two opposing rows show actual cards only, maximum five per side. Front/back coordinates remain in saves/rules; placement and once-per-round movement use two clear controls. Upgrades still require the paid matching copy. No tactical coordinates are discarded.
- Own splash portraits for all spirits, square atlas crop inside proportional card frames; no animated model required on the production battlefield and no unit food photo. Collection/deck/pack portraits use the same corrected crop component. Existing model files/resolvers remain for compatibility.
- Commander portrait, HP, mana gems, contextual written commands and a distinct face-down trap card; valid commander targets attach to the portrait. Summon/set/flip/lunge/impact/cast name/ultimate/recipe cues, elemental paths and actual HP/shield floats. Root/freeze/burn/shield markers are inspectable; heal/control/status rings follow engine transitions.
- `rootedUntil`/`burn`/event `impacts` are optional additive checkpoint data. Root trap keeps its 20 damage and duration, now explicitly prevents movement as well as attack/ultimate. Thaw clears root/control without refunding the spent attack. Embers adds a non-stacking 4-damage burn for two end-of-round ticks; shields absorb first. Death/discard/passives occur once, revival cleans old statuses. This is a trap balance change requiring human playtest.
- Recorded impacts distinguish a granted 20 shield from the subsequent 6-shield hit even when the final shield total rises. Final regression verifies both authoritative hit metadata and checkpoint validation. Legacy snapshots without impacts still derive negative deltas.
