## Changes in player behavior

- Preset player lineup removed from production entry. Cards in the equipped deck become the seeded hand; only legal paid plays populate the board.
- Full catalog accessible; card text describes the actual Tactical conversion, with differentiated entry/death/spell passives, counters, stars and school recipe.
- Separate 5-card pack screen reuses original art, shows rates/guarantees before opening and stores results before reveal. New random TCG packs follow the owner's latest explicit gacha request, superseding the older no-random-combat-pack master plan restriction.
- Campaign has objectives, before/after narrative, earned rewards, a choice and two ending passages. Existing Manga artwork is preserved and unlocks are mapped; no newly drawn Manga is claimed.
- Cooperating UI writes use Web Locks when available and retain optimistic readback/rollback otherwise. This is local progress, not multi-device synchronization or an authoritative economy.

## Failures and limits

Initial legal-command runs exposed a too-hard opening deck and tie ordering favoring enemy IDs. Novice AI decks, a disclosed 2-mana opening handicap in selected PvE encounters, alternating tie initiative and a starter with a real trap were authored and retested. Earlier failed attempts are not counted as passes.

Browser review remains BLOCKED: local Playwright's Chromium download returned invalid/truncated archives, and the cloud browser could not connect to the localhost server. SSR, pure simulations and the successful compiled package do not establish visual/mobile/touch/audio/FPS acceptance. Physical-device, independent art/rights, online PvP and production approval remain open; no production-100% claim.
