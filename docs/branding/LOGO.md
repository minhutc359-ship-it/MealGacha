# Soul of Meal — logo v3.1

Biểu tượng mới: bát cơm, linh hỏa như cánh sen, hai lá bài. Tông vàng/ivory/teal, nền alpha thật. Tên app được typeset bằng HTML để sắc nét ở mọi kích thước.

Tạo bằng built-in `image_gen.imagegen`; original: `exec-c74ef8a1-256b-4975-a1e1-1e44552b90d0.png`. Chỉ resize/encode kỹ thuật bằng Pillow; không vẽ lại. Asset dùng trong app: `public/assets/brand/soul-of-meal.webp`, icon PNG 192/512, apple-touch-icon 180 và favicon ICO nhiều kích thước.

Prompt nguyên bản:

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: transparent app emblem for a Vietnamese food fantasy card game called Soul of Meal.
Primary request: Create one original, memorable premium game logo symbol: a small elegant ceramic rice bowl cradling a warm golden spirit flame shaped like three flowing lotus petals, with two subtly overlapping collectible-card silhouettes behind the bowl. The flame is the main focal point, suggesting living cooking memories. No lettering; the title will be typeset in the app.
Style: crisp stylized anime RPG emblem, softly shaded 3D enamel illustration with clean vector-like contours, strong readable silhouette at 32px, restrained details. Polished rather than busy.
Palette: warm antique gold and amber flame, ivory rice/bowl accents, deep teal card surfaces, dark navy outlines, fits both a dark fantasy battle UI and a cream food discovery UI.
Composition: centered single compact square emblem, front view, generous transparent padding, emblem occupies about 80% of canvas; isolated with genuine transparent alpha.
Constraints: no text, no watermark, no mockup, no background, no frame around the whole canvas, no tiny decorative flourishes, no copyrighted game characters. Clear bowl + spirit flame + card identity.
```
