# Adventure 4.9 archive

The tile overworld, one-on-one UI, walking atlases and 4.8 terrain/prop atlases are retained here for history and recovery. They are no longer shipped in public/ or imported by the game. Source snapshots use .txt to stay outside the product/typecheck.

Legacy Adventure save types, map identifiers, reducers and parser validation remain in src/game for v1/v2 save compatibility. Old captures, coins, inventories, quests and in-progress encounters remain in the same save field. The 5.0 RPG reads their progress once without rewriting it. Historical QA scripts apply to the old release, not 5.0.
